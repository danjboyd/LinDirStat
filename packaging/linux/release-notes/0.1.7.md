Changed, following WinDirStat:

- Size units are named as WinDirStat names them: KiB, MiB, GiB and TiB
  ("22.32 MiB"). The values are the same as before (they were always
  1024-based); only the names change.
- Sizes under 1 KiB read "40 bytes", translated as in WinDirStat.
- Sizes and percentages use your locale's decimal separator, and
  percentages have two decimals, as in WinDirStat: "89.57%" in English,
  "22,32 MiB" and "89,57%" in German.
  On macOS the separator follows System Settings › General › Language &
  Region (Number format), even when LinDirStat itself is in English.

Fixed:

- On macOS, the sorted "Physical Size" header in Largest Files is no longer
  cut to "Physical Si…".
- Select Target's Total and Free columns are wide enough for two-decimal
  sizes; they were cut to "603.95…" in 0.1.6.

Behind the scenes:

- The WinDirStat parity checklist is up to date with what has shipped.
- Every change to LinDirStat must now pass the Linux and macOS builds and
  tests before it can be merged.
