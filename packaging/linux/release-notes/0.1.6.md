New, following WinDirStat 2.9:

- Two new treemap layouts, Hilbert and Moore, in Preferences › Treemap ›
  Style (Settings on macOS) beside KDirStat and SequoiaView. They place
  each folder's items along a space-filling curve, so neighbouring items
  stay next to each other and the cells keep a squarish shape.

Changed:

- Sizes have two decimals, as in WinDirStat: "1.50 KB", "36.96 MB",
  "2.50 GB". A size switches to the next unit just below it, so it never
  shows "1024.00 KB". Sizes under 1 KB stay in bytes. For exact byte
  counts, turn off Preferences › General › Show sizes in human-readable
  format.

Fixed:

- Selecting a file too small to be drawn in the treemap now frames the
  nearest folder that is drawn, as in WinDirStat. Before, the frame was a
  dot or nothing at all. This applies to every layout.

Behind the scenes:

- Packages are built with gnustep-packager 0.1.6.
- Every change to LinDirStat is now checked on Linux by its GUI parity
  gate: the whole window, with the Adwaita theme, in several states. The
  treemap in all four layouts still matches WinDirStat's pixel for pixel.
